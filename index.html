The program duration is 2 years across 4 semesters and carries 102 credits.
You can build knowledge across core business areas, strengthen your understanding of business decisions and develop skills relevant to broader responsibilities. Your career outcomes depend on your experience, performance, skills and available opportunities.
No course can guarantee a promotion or salary increase. An MBA can help you develop business knowledge and prepare for opportunities, but outcomes depend on several factors including your experience, performance and the role or market.
Applicants need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Amity's published program information includes minimum marks and additional conditions; the admissions team can guide you through the criteria applicable to your qualification.
Choose based on your interests, current experience and the direction you want to explore. Options include Marketing and Sales Management, Finance and Accounting Management, Business Analytics, Digital Marketing Management, Human Resource Management, IT Management and General Management, among others.
Amity lists career resources such as AI resume building, mock interviews, 1:1 industry mentorship, LinkedIn analysis, skill development and placement assistance. These services support preparation but do not guarantee employment.
